## Selected primary confirmatory endpoint

The selected binary primary endpoint is **evidence-supported correct audit completion by 180 seconds**.

A trial can succeed only when all of the following are true:

1. every prespecified required judgment for that case is correct;
2. every required judgment has at least one prespecified acceptable evidence/support set for the reviewer's assigned condition under the frozen case contract;
3. the submitted answer does not add a material set-valued fact absent from gold and does not submit a mismatched authorization state;
4. the trial is completed by 180 seconds under the separately selected and frozen primary timing clock;
5. a deadline miss/timeout cannot count as success.

The required judgments and acceptable support sets are represented case-by-case by the analysis-side `AR-P003-v0.3-primary-endpoint-case-v0.1` contract and must be frozen before confirmatory execution.

## Separate safety endpoint

**Critical false clearance** remains a separate prespecified safety endpoint.

The development contract can flag a critical false clearance when a case-specific prespecified check is applicable and the answer incorrectly clears that adverse state. Current supported rules include:

- true authorization violation reported false;
- non-empty critical incident set reported empty;
- adverse verification state reported as `confirmed` or `not_required`;
- important missing evidence reported absent when gold says it is present.

This safety endpoint is reported separately from the binary primary endpoint. Its acceptable threshold or maximum increase remains unresolved under `effect_precision_target`.

## Development-only contingency

Before confirmatory freeze only, development testing may show that the 180-second endpoint is technically invalid or practically unusable, including timer failure, unreliable browser/device behavior, an irreproducible judgment/support rule, or evidence that the threshold cannot support a meaningful professional-reviewer study.

If that occurs:

1. **STOP**;
2. do not inspect confirmatory outcomes;
3. do not automatically promote the component endpoint set;
4. return to methodology-decision state;
5. require an explicit owner decision, a new protocol version, updated power/planning, updated freeze-readiness, and updated hashes/artifacts.

A later owner decision may revise the fixed deadline or select `component_endpoint_set`. Once the confirmatory protocol is frozen or confirmatory data collection begins, the primary endpoint cannot be changed merely because results are unfavorable. If timing later becomes technically unusable, the prespecified primary result must be reported as affected/non-estimable rather than retroactively replacing it with a secondary endpoint.

## Evidence boundary

This is methodology preparation and synthetic engineering validation only. It is not human evidence and does not establish an audit-accuracy, speed, safety, compliance, or productivity benefit.
