# AR-P003 v0.3 — Reviewer-Population Decision

**Decision date:** 2026-09-21

**Decision ID:** `reviewer_population`

**Selected candidate:** `relevant_professional_reviewers`

**Authority:** explicit project-owner decision by Blake Gaucher

**Protocol version:** `v0.3-draft-2026-09-21-professional-reviewers-v0.1`

## Decision

The target population is **relevant professional reviewers** with at least 1 year of relevant professional or practical experience in one or more of:

- technical audit;
- cybersecurity;
- compliance;
- AI governance;
- incident investigation or incident review;
- software or system operations;
- technical assurance;
- closely related evidence-review work.

Record one total relevant-experience band for each reviewer: **1–2 years**, **3–5 years**, or **6+ years**.

Reviewers must have sufficient English proficiency to understand the technical evidence, instructions, and structured response interface. No particular degree, certification, or job title is required when the practical-experience criterion is met. Prior general familiarity with AI Activity Receipt is permitted and must be recorded.

## Exclusions

Exclude anyone who:

1. constructed or materially edited the sealed evaluation cases;
2. created or had access to hidden gold answers;
3. participated in scoring-rule development using the sealed cases;
4. scored the confirmatory responses;
5. had access to protected confirmatory analysis material before completing their assigned cases.

## Study-case assistance rule

During study cases, reviewers may not use:

- external web search;
- external AI assistants;
- another person;
- outside tools or evidence not supplied by the study.

Only an explicitly versioned protocol amendment made before confirmatory execution may authorize an exception.

## Rationale and inference boundary

AI Activity Receipt is intended for consequential AI activity review and governance workflows. A relevant professional population provides a more defensible test of the intended audit/evidence-review use than a general convenience population. Multiple related professional domains improve applicability across that intended workflow, while the experience bands preserve transparent reporting.

Recruitment and later claims must remain scoped to the professional population actually recruited. A convenience or unrepresentative sample must not be generalized to all auditors, cybersecurity professionals, compliance professionals, AI-governance professionals, or other broader groups.
