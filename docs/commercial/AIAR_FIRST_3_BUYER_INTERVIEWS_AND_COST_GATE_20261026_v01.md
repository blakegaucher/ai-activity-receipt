## Bounded 12-minute first interview guide (NOT SENT)

1. “Think of a recent AI-assisted document or report review where someone had to verify what happened. What records did you actually rely on?” Record explicit workflow and source of recollection.
2. “Where was the evidence missing, disputed, or slow to reconstruct? What was the impact, even if it was negligible?” Allow **no problem** response.
3. “How do you document the human approval and external confirmation of consequential actions today? Who decides whether the current process is acceptable?” Avoid conflating a system 'sent' event with external delivery.
4. “Who would decide whether to pay for one bounded retrospective evaluation? What else would you buy or use instead?” Obtain buyer and alternatives, not just enthusiasm.
5. “Would you consider a one-time fixed-fee evidence reconstruction on a single **authorized, de-identified** workflow? At what fee would you decline? What range would be credible?” If they refuse to price, record **REFUSED/UNKNOWN**, not $0.
6. “What would have to be demonstrated in a tiny, measurable pilot before you authorize spending?” Record objections and exact future condition, not fake commitments.

Avoid encouraging the interviewee to claim need, value or compliance. Date/source and an exact quote for any material statement; permission before storing names; keep identifiable notes private.
