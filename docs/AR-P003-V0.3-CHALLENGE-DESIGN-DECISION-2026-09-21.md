# AR-P003 v0.3 — Challenge-Design Decision

> Decision date: 2026-09-21  
> Decision authority: project owner  
> Status: selected methodology decision; development-only, not frozen, not executed.

## Selected candidate

`integrated_challenge_strata`

Use stale, incomplete, and conflicting Receipt challenge strata inside the same broader crossed study as ordinary cases. Do not create a separate robustness cohort.

## Rationale

This preserves the robustness question without creating a second recruitment, cohort, randomization, and power framework. Ordinary and challenge results remain separately reported.

## Compatibility with the selected comparison design

Challenge cases remain inside the selected three-condition architecture:

1. raw evidence;
2. neutral structured event table plus the same evidence;
3. Activity Receipt plus the same evidence.

The structured-control artifact is derived from the canonical Activity Record, not from the Receipt artifact. Receipt-specific corruption state must not be copied into the neutral structured-control layer. Underlying evidence remains shared across conditions.

## Preserved historical conflict

The repository historically used integrated challenge strata. The earlier research review recommended `separate_robustness_cohort`. That rejected alternative and the source conflict remain preserved in the methodology ledger and decision-support artifacts.

## Still not frozen

This selection does not freeze final challenge counts, allocation proportions, reviewer/case counts, assignment seed, power/precision plan, final sealed cases, or final leakage/manual review.

Therefore issue #47's challenge-strata checkbox remains open.
