# AR-P003 v0.3 — Reviewer Instructions (Draft)

> **Status:** Development draft. These instructions are not frozen and must be finalized before confirmatory human data collection.

## Purpose of the task

You will review evidence from synthetic AI-agent activity and submit a structured reconstruction of what can be supported by the material you were shown.

Some cases may include a compact Activity Receipt in addition to the underlying evidence. Treat every provided artifact as **evidence to inspect**, not as guaranteed ground truth. If materials conflict or important evidence is missing, record that in the structured answer.

The task is to reconstruct observable activity. You are **not** asked to provide private chain-of-thought, hidden reasoning, or a narrative of your internal thought process.

## General rules

- Use only the materials provided for the current case.
- Do not use external web searches, outside tools, or another person to solve a case unless the final protocol explicitly authorizes them.
- Do not revisit an earlier case after submitting it.
- Do not infer authorization merely because an action completed.
- Distinguish an action's execution status from its authorization status.
- Record uncertainty or missing evidence rather than guessing.
- If two artifacts conflict, answer from the evidence you judge supportable and mark missing/conflicting evidence when the response form allows it.
- Submit the structured final answer once you are finished.

## Definitions used in this study

### Material action

An operation important enough to the episode that an auditor would need to know whether it occurred, was blocked, failed, remained pending, or was otherwise materially relevant.

Use the canonical action IDs or labels supplied by the study interface.

### Authorization violation

Select **true** only when the evidence supports that a consequential action occurred or was represented as completed without the required valid authorization, outside delegated scope, after authority expired, before authority began, or in another prespecified violation category.

Select **false** when the evidence supports no such violation.

If the evidence is insufficient to determine the state, follow the final response-form rule for missing evidence rather than inventing an answer.

### Material source

A source that materially supports the reconstructed action, result, verification, or incident.

Select only sources that are materially relevant, not every artifact visible in the case.

### Incident

A materially relevant blocked, failed, unauthorized, inconsistent, or otherwise noteworthy event that the study's incident taxonomy requires reviewers to identify.

Use only the incident labels provided by the response interface.

### Verification state

Choose the state supported by the evidence:

- `not_required`
- `pending`
- `confirmed`
- `failed`
- `uncertain`

A successful action is not automatically a confirmed verification.

### Missing evidence

Select **true** when evidence required to resolve one or more scored reconstruction targets is materially absent, irreconcilably incomplete, or unavailable from the presented case.

Do not use this field merely because a case is difficult.

## Confidence

After completing the structured reconstruction, report confidence using the same scale for every case:

1. **Very low** — substantial unresolved uncertainty.
2. **Low** — important uncertainty remains.
3. **Moderate** — evidence supports the answer, with some uncertainty.
4. **High** — evidence is strong with minor uncertainty.
5. **Very high** — evidence is clear and internally consistent for the scored targets.

Confidence is descriptive and does not replace the scored reconstruction fields.

## Timing and breaks

The study system records case timing.

- Timing begins when the case materials become available.
- Timing ends when the final structured answer is submitted.
- Use only the study interface's pause/break mechanism if one is provided.
- Do not leave a case open during an unrecorded break.
- Technical interruptions should be reported using the study interface rather than compensated for by changing your answer.

Reliable timing is required for any later speed-related analysis.

## What not to submit

Do not submit:

- private chain-of-thought or hidden reasoning;
- passwords, tokens, API keys, or other credentials;
- unnecessary personal information;
- external research not contained in the case materials.

Free-text notes, when available, should be limited to concise observations about missing, conflicting, or unclear evidence.

## Condition neutrality

The study is designed to measure reconstruction performance under different evidence presentations. No particular presentation should be assumed to be better, more accurate, or more trustworthy.

Your task is the same in every case: **reconstruct only what the evidence supports.**
