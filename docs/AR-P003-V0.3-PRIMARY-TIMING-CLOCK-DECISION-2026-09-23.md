## Owner decision

**Decision ID:** `primary_timing_clock`

**Selected candidate:** `wall_deadline_with_hidden_sensitivity`

The selected primary timing definition for `correct_completion_by_180s` is **elapsed wall time**.

## Primary rule

1. The primary deadline is 180 seconds of wall elapsed time.
2. Background/hidden elapsed time remains inside the primary deadline; it is not subtracted.
3. A trial over the 180-second wall deadline cannot satisfy the primary endpoint.
4. Active elapsed time remains a secondary/sensitivity and diagnostic measure.
5. Trials with more than 10 seconds hidden are reserved for a prespecified sensitivity analysis; the sensitivity result cannot replace the primary wall-clock result after outcomes are observed.
6. Slow, difficult, incorrect, or unfavorable responses are not exclusions merely because they fail the deadline.
7. Exact technical-failure, reload/context-break, timeout/last-answer capture, and allowed browser/device rules remain pre-execution freeze requirements.

## Rationale

The already-selected primary endpoint is evidence-supported correct completion **by 180 seconds**. Wall time is the direct operational interpretation of that fixed deadline. It also avoids making primary success depend on manual pause behavior, tab visibility, or pause-detection implementation.

The development system preserves both wall and active elapsed time, so this selection does not discard timing observability. Active time remains useful for sensitivity analysis.

`active_time_primary` remains preserved as the non-selected historical/development alternative. It is not deleted or reinterpreted.
