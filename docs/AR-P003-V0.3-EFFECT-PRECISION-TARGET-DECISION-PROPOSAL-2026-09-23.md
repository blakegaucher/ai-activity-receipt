## Proposed decision

Select:

`effect_precision_target = research_review_proposed_thresholds`

with the following project-specific preregistration decision assumptions:

1. **+5 percentage points** absolute supported-completion improvement as the smallest practically important Receipt-specific benefit.
2. **3 percentage points** as the maximum accuracy loss permitted by a separate noninferiority analysis if a speed claim is ever pursued.
3. **+2 percentage points** as the maximum acceptable absolute increase in critical false clearance.

These values are owner/project decision thresholds. They are **not industry norms**, not estimates from AR-P003 v0.2.3, and not evidence that the true effects equal these values.

## Contrast mapping for the three-condition design

The selected three-condition architecture exists to distinguish generic organization benefit from Receipt-specific incremental benefit.

For effect-target planning:

- `receipt_vs_structured` is the direct Receipt-specific incremental contrast and is the proposed contrast to which the +5 percentage-point minimum practically important benefit applies.
- `receipt_vs_raw` remains an important total-presentation contrast and should be reported as prespecified supporting evidence.
- `structured_vs_raw` remains the generic-structuring/context contrast and should be reported separately.

This mapping does not convert either supporting contrast into a hidden post-hoc primary claim.

## Critical-false-clearance guardrail

Critical false clearance remains a separate safety endpoint, not part of the binary primary endpoint.

The proposed +2 percentage-point guardrail should be checked for Receipt relative to both relevant comparators:

- Receipt vs structured; and
- Receipt vs raw.

A favorable primary supported-completion result should not be presented as an unqualified benefit if the prespecified safety analysis shows a Receipt-associated critical-false-clearance increase above the accepted guardrail.

The exact inferential procedure and uncertainty rule for the safety endpoint still must be frozen in the final analysis plan.

## Separate speed-claim margin

The proposed 3 percentage-point noninferiority margin is **dormant unless a separate speed claim is preregistered**.

A speed claim would require, before execution:

- a separately defined accuracy-preservation endpoint;
- the 3 percentage-point noninferiority margin applied to that endpoint;
- a prespecified inferential rule;
- reliable timing under the selected wall-clock design.

Selecting this candidate does not itself authorize or require a speed claim.

## Why this is the next defensible decision

The repository cannot complete crossed-design power/precision planning while the minimum effect or precision target remains unspecified.

The earlier v0.2.3 auxiliary evidence cannot estimate a human effect because it used an auxiliary AI reviewer, reached ceiling non-timing results, and had no usable human timing distribution. Using the observed auxiliary result to choose a favorable human threshold would therefore be inappropriate.

The preserved research-review thresholds are instead explicit, pre-outcome decision assumptions. Selecting them now would let the crossed-design planner evaluate feasibility without inspecting confirmatory outcomes.
